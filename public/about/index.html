DROOM.IRIS
ABOUT

Hi I'm iris,
I'm a freelance graphic designer / visual artist and DJ based in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. I work under the pseudonym; Droom.iris, which is inspired by my dreamlike take on shapes, typography, rhythms and vibrant colour palettes.

I focus on the design of visual identities, systems and colourful posters. I'm mostly inspired by graphics from the 80's and I love experimenting with different techniques, both analog and digital.
I'm also involved with the Riso Wizards, a local Riso-publisher based in Rotterdam.

I'm available for freelance work, contact me for enquiries!
