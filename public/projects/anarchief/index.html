anarchief

Identity design for Roodkapje's anarchief, or, the spirit of Happenings transformed. It currently takes the shape of an exhibition consisting of anarchival pieces and traces that evolve from the Happenings organised by the residents of the Hamburger Community, a residency located in Roodkapje Rotterdam. While the traces are more like leftovers or small documentations of the Happenings, the pieces are commissioned works where artists are asked to anarchive their experience of a Happening.
